Sounds in this folder
=====================

The files steps_snow_01.ogg to steps_snow_12.ogg are cut and level-adjusted from one recording published under CC0 1.0 Universal
(Public Domain Dedication):

  "Footstep in the Snow #2" by Joseph SARDIN, BigSoundBank
  https://bigsoundbank.com/footstep-in-the-snow-2-s2890.html

CC0 1.0 in short: the author waives all copyright and related rights in the work to the extent the law allows, so that anyone may
copy, change, distribute and use it, also commercially, without asking and without attribution. Credit is given anyway.

Full legal code: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode
Summary:         https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

SOUNDS.json lists, for every file here, its source, author, licence line, the archived copy of the source page, the date the page
was read and how the file was made. The slicing is done by tools/audio/slice_steps.py.
