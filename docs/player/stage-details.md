# The delta-v tools in the staging stack

**For:** players who look at the stages' details in KSP's staging stack.
**You need:** ReDefinition installed.
**You get:** how the delta-v tools and the stage details work with ReDefinition.

## Open the tools

Press KSP's *Δv* button at the bottom of the screen. Every stage opens its details, and
in the VAB and the SPH the *Δv Tools* window slides in with them, under the lowest stage
and above the vessel's total delta-v. Press the button again, and the details and the
window slide out.

The window has what the numbers are worked out for:

| Row | Sets |
|---|---|
| *Body* | the body the vessel starts from |
| *Atmosphere* | *Sea Level*, *Altitude* or *Vacuum* |
| *Altitude* | the height for *Altitude*, typed or on the slider |
| *Pressure* | shows the air pressure there |

The window stays at the bottom when the stages scroll, and is as wide as the stages with
their details. In flight KSP works the stages out for where the vessel is, so the button
opens and closes the details only.

A click on a stage's number still opens or closes that stage's details alone.

## The details

Each stage shows its Isp, thrust, TWR, start and end mass, and burn time, always all of
them, in two columns side by side. Its delta-v stands under the stage's number, as in
KSP.
