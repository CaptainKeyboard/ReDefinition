# Stage details in two columns

**For:** players who show more than a few values beside the stages in KSP's staging stack.
**You need:** ReDefinition installed.
**You get:** how the stage details are laid out.

KSP's staging stack can show details beside each stage: Isp, thrust, TWR, start and end
mass, and burn time. Choose them in KSP's *Δv Tools*, under *Detailed Stage Information*.
KSP lists them one below the other, so every stage grows taller with each value. The
stage's delta-v is not among them: KSP shows it under each stage's number.

With more than four values chosen, ReDefinition lists them in two columns side by side:
the first half on the left, the rest on the right. Each stage is then as tall as four
values and twice as wide. With four or fewer, the details stay in one column as in KSP.
They slide in and out as in KSP.

Where the wider stages reach under the *Δv Tools* window, the window moves left of them,
and follows them as they slide.

This works in the VAB, the SPH and in flight.
