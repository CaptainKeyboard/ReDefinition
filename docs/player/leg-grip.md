# Grip for landing legs

**For:** players whose landers slide down slopes.
**You need:** ReDefinition and ModuleManager installed. No graphics profile is needed.
**You get:** how to give a landing leg more grip, as a grip pad has.

Every landing leg has a *Traction* button in its part menu in the VAB and the SPH, as the
grip pads have. Each click steps to the next grip, and the button shows the one set:

| Traction | Grip against KSP's |
|---|---|
| *Default* | as in KSP |
| *Medium Grip* | 1.25 times |
| *High Grip* | 1.9 times |
| *Extreme Grip* | 2.75 times |

The factors are those of the grip pads' materials against the default material. A
click changes the legs placed in symmetry as well.

A leg grips through its foot's wheel, not like a grip pad through its surface, so the
same choice holds a leg and a pad alike only roughly. Legs from other mods get the button
where KSP holds their grip fixed, as it does for its own.

Without ModuleManager the button does not appear, and the legs grip as in KSP.
